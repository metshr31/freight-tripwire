# Canada and Mexico monitoring

The watcher checks public truckload, LTL, intermodal/rail and port sources in English, French and Spanish. A port authority reader is not coverage of every terminal operator. Corporate or parent-company sources are not all customer advisories or subsidiary-specific releases. Mode tags identify the entity scope, not a guarantee that every development will be published by that reader.

## Freshness and timing

- Monday–Friday, every 20 minutes from 8:00 a.m. through 7:00 p.m. America/New_York. Existing schedule unchanged.
- Maximum publication age: 120 minutes. The publication timestamp must include a timezone. Original-page publisher metadata is consulted when a primary-source item lacks a usable feed timestamp (bounded lookups).
- Old stories resurfacing in indexes, new discovery of old URLs, date-only records, and undated records do not generate news pushes. Previously queued stories expire as they age out.
- First reads create quiet baselines. No historical backlog is sent.
- Inline expansion notices and public documents are readable, but they cannot generate news pushes until a trustworthy recent publication time is available. A successful reader and an eligible fresh alert are separate measures.
- Passenger/cruise headlines are excluded from added port readers. Association statements are supplementary; they do not count as direct readers for each member carrier.
